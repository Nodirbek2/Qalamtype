# Recorded engine sound sources

All source recordings are CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Final game assets are edited, equalized, looped, normalized and modestly pitch adjusted, with some recordings mixed together. No synthesized engine oscillators or AI-generated audio is audible. These are game sound designs; the files are not claimed to be exact recordings of each named garage model.

- domasx2 — racing car engine sound loops (loop_0.wav), based on a public-domain car recording. https://opengameart.org/content/racing-car-engine-sound-loops
- PritzProductions — Ferrari 360 Spider - engine sound. Recorded directly above the engine. https://freesound.org/people/PritzProductions/sounds/241083/
- florianreichelt — Sound of a Lamborghini Engine Start up and Kick Down. https://freesound.org/people/florianreichelt/sounds/457559/
- myeclecticself — Car Engine: Maserati GranTurismo Drive By 2, 2018 V8 in Sport Mode. https://freesound.org/people/myeclecticself/sounds/585214/

Tiko/Jiguli/Spark: different processed versions of the sedan recording. Nexia 1/2: sedan with small Ferrari/Maserati layers. Lacetti: Ferrari/sedan. Cobalt: Maserati/sedan. Gentra/Malibu: different Ferrari segments. Tahoe: Lamborghini. G-wagon: Lamborghini/Maserati mix.
